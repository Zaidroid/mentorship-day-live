# Day of runbook

## Before doors open

1. On the computer that drives the wall, open Chrome in kiosk mode so videos can play with sound:
   `chrome --kiosk --autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required https://mentorship-day-live.netlify.app/screen/`
   Without the flag, click once anywhere on the screen after it loads.
2. On the remote, tap Screen check. The red frame and blue corners must sit exactly on the edges of the wall, and the small sample text must be readable from the back of the hall. Then tap Live screen.
3. Set the This Way directions in the remote, as seen from the screen.
4. Scan the QR code with a phone, answer the poll, and confirm the word appears on the screen.
5. Check the Screen Feed sheet: Checked in, WiFi name and password, and Announcement if needed.

## The remote

Open https://mentorship-day-live.netlify.app/remote/ on a phone, type your name and the team PIN.

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Screen | Automatic or hold a mode, hold one scene, move speaker cards, play or stop videos, send an announcement, black or logo screen, screen check, light or dark, This Way directions |
| Approve | Approve or decline photos and Networking Board profiles. Remove anything already on the screen |
| Polls | Open a poll, close it, clear its answers, hide a word, add a new poll |
| Log | Who did what, and when |

Changes reach the screen within a few seconds.

## Moderation

* Check the Approve tab every few minutes. The tab shows a number when something is waiting.
* Decline anything off topic. Remove from the wall anything that should not be there.
* Tap a word in the Polls tab to hide it from the screen. Tap again to bring it back.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Remote does not respond | Use the keyboard on the screen computer: H shows the keys. 0 automatic, 1 to 7 modes, N next scene, K screen check, B black, L logos, V stop video, F full screen |
| Video has no sound | Click once on the screen, then play it again from the remote |
| Internet drops | The screen keeps running on the last data it received and the built in schedule. The ticker shows "Offline, saved data" |
| Screen looks wrong after a change | Reload the page. It picks up the current state straight away |
