# Team Card

For the registration desk and the floor team, Tuesday 17 November 2026.

## At Registration, One Sentence

**"Scan the screen and find your name."**

Then they confirm with the last 4 digits of the mobile number they registered with. That is all.

## When Sign In Does Not Work

| The phone says, or they say | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Their name does not come up | Type at least 3 letters of the first or last name, as they registered. Still not there: tap "I am not on the list" and continue as a guest. Guests can do everything; their card and takeaways show after the team checks them |
| "That is not the right number. Try again." | It is the mobile number they registered with, the last 4 digits. The phone shows how many tries are left |
| "Too many tries. Ask the team at the registration desk." | Open the remote, People tab, Locked Out. Check it is their name, tap Unlock. They try again |
| "This name is signed in on 3 phones already." | Unlock in the People tab clears that too |
| They do not know which number they used | The remote never shows numbers. They continue as a guest |
| They do not want to sign in | "Not now". The page works without signing in |
| They ask about one to ones | They book at the registration desk |

## Alerts

If the team has an alert chat on Telegram, messages say a count and the remote link, never a name. Each kind comes at most once every 3 minutes. The same things show at the top of the remote's Status tab. N below is the count.

| Alert | What it means | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "N photos are waiting for approval." | Attendees sent photos | Remote, Approve tab: Approve or Decline |
| "N cards or takeaways are waiting for approval." | Mostly from guests | Remote, Approve tab: Approve or Decline |
| "Someone is locked out of sign in and needs Unlock." | A name reached 10 wrong tries | Remote, People tab, Locked Out: check the name, Unlock. A lock on one phone shows in the People tab without an alert |
| "The wall reported an error." | The wall page had a problem | Tell the person at the wall laptop. The Status tab shows the error |
| "N wrong PINs in the last 10 minutes." | Someone may be guessing the team PIN | Tell the person in charge |
